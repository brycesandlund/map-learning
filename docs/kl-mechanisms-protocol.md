Protocol and adaptive-decision record, November 10, 2026. Authorized to distinguish the causes of improved exploration. Predeclared protocol before scientific runs:

- All arms fix repulsion α=.03, latent dimension8, unchanged encoder/dynamics architecture and optimization. β is 0 or1e-5. Mean inference in all queues. No tuning based on new seed outcomes.
- Core 2×2: deterministic means versus prescribed sampled noise, crossed with no KL versus learned-prior KL. Additional current adaptive-posterior winner and adaptive β=0 controls. Prior-adaptation arms: winner with prior LR×3, LR×10, or five total prior optimizer steps per joint step. Nine arms total. Extra prior steps use the same detached pre-update KL batch, with no encoder/dynamics gradient or extra data sample.
- The prescribed isotropic standard deviation schedule is RMS posterior standard deviation averaged over previous winning seeds0/1/2. Fixed before these runs, linearly interpolated in gradient-update count; source observations and results of new runs do not affect it. This ablation does not reproduce the winner's heteroscedastic posterior or gradients through its variance head. A fixed-noise+KL arm makes the noise×KL comparison factorial; the adaptive winner separately tests the coupled setup.
- Shared data: two families of archived experience (learned winner and stochastic β=0), source seeds6/7/8. All models start from scratch at paired seeds9/10/11. Feed each recorded stream chronologically, exposing500 new transitions before each event and using identical shuffled epoch slices and repulsion samples. 100k replay transitions,4,112 joint updates,1,050,000 presentations/head. No new actions are collected for training. 54 fits total. Source family and source/model seed pair are blocking variables, not54 independent environments.

Online feedback is free to change data; compare its outcomes with shared-data results. If follow-ups are warranted, label them separately.
